📖 Complete Setup & User Guide

📱 Step 1: Connect Your Android Phone over ADB

1. On your phone, go to Settings > About Phone and tap Build Number 7 times to enable Developer Options.
2. In Developer Options, enable USB Debugging (and "Stay Awake" while charging).
3. Plug your phone into your Mac/PC via USB cable, or connect via Wi-Fi: adb connect <phone-ip>:5555.
4. When prompted on your phone screen, check "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow.

🤖 Step 2: Get a Free Google Gemini API Key

1. Visit Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com).
2. Sign in with any Google account and click Create API key (100% Free tier).
3. Copy your key, paste it into the Google Gemini API card on this dashboard, and click Save Key.
4. Click ⚡ Test Key to verify your live AI connection.

🔑 Step 3: Synchronize Live Referral Keys

Click the orange Web Sync button on the Master Keys Pool card above.
The dashboard connects directly to the official cloud vault at nusukkeys.wajed.bd and downloads over 82,000+ verified referral codes instantly!

🚀 Step 4: Start Autonomous Automation

1. Open the Nusuk app on your Android phone.
2. Tap the menu, scroll down to Competitions & Challenges, and tap Play Now!.
3. On this dashboard, click ▶ START AUTONOMOUS LOOP.
4. The engine will automatically solve questions using the 377+ built-in knowledge base + Gemini AI, redeem referral keys whenever your balance is low, and take human-like breather pauses between batches!

🛡️ Built-in Anti-Ban Features: AMOLED screen dimming (saves battery & burn-in), randomized response jitter (0.8s - 2.5s), automatic 30s rest breaks after each 20-question batch, and instant emergency stop button.
